# Research synthesis — 2026-08-11

Condensed evidence base grounding the Session 3 roadmap (slip analytics,
forecast-call prep, coaching digests, pattern detection). Two independent
research passes converged: an adversarially-verified deep-research run
(this synthesis) and an external report
(`external-research-2026-08-11.md`).

**Epistemic status: every figure below is a correlational vendor benchmark.
Cite as "unaudited benchmarks", never causal. Never claim hygiene causes
wins.**

## Converged conclusions

- The moat is the **snapshot history** — CRMs cannot derive slippage, aging,
  or forecast-category drift from a single export.
- The packaging is **forecast-call prep + private coaching digests**,
  dollar-weighted. Raw flag counts are the commodity.
- The full exception list survives only as a drill-down appendix, never the
  headline.

## Evidence appendix

- **Ebsta × Pavilion 2024** (4.2M opps): 44% of deals slipped; slipped win
  rates −67% (worst >8 weeks); close date moved >3× → win rate −77%; deal 50%
  past org's historical stage duration → 120% likelier to slip; close within
  historical stage norms → +203% win rate; opps updated weekly → +17% close
  rate. (2025 edition: slippage 36%; its "−113%" figure is mathematically
  impossible — treat directionally.)
- **Gong Labs** (n=13,439): closed-WON deals update close dates 107% more
  often / fluctuate 31% more than lost — movement ≠ risk; large (≥3-week)
  pushes and frozen dates are the risk signals.
- **CSO Insights / MySalesCoach**: active coaching +21.3% quota attainment,
  +19% win rate; weekly coaching 76% attainment vs 47% quarterly. Private
  digests, never leaderboards (bottom-rank publishing raises attrition).
- **Practitioner accountability loop** (Ignacio et al.): repeated nudges with
  a counter, manager loop-in at ~3 unresolved cycles — flags need memory.
- **Coverage**: required multiple ≈ 1 / win rate (2x–7x by segment); blind 3x
  is a relic.
- **Refuted in verification (do not cite)**: "Einstein only 52% accurate" and
  related opacity anecdotes; "regular pipeline reviews → 15% higher win
  rates". Competitive positioning rests on price/data-ownership/CRM-agnostic
  structural facts instead (Clari $24k–$120k+ contracts; Scratchpad/Weflow
  Salesforce-only; Dooly shutdown 2025 with 30-day data retention).

## Guardrails carried into the build

- No ML/black-box scores. Deterministic, auditable rules only.
- No public leaderboards, rankings, or gamification of hygiene — private
  digests only.
- No CRM write-back, no seller-facing nudges: agents inspect, people sell.
- No everything-dashboard growth; one-screen views, dollar-weighted.
- Frequent small close-date updates must not trip anything (Gong nuance);
  the risk signals are serial/LARGE pushes and frozen dates.
